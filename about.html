Who We Are

Built on Belief

Vihaan Constructions was founded in 2022 by a group of construction professionals who shared a common frustration: that most builders prioritised speed over substance, and scale over sincerity.

We set out to do things differently. We started small — our first project was a 1,800 sq.ft. retail store in T. Nagar — and we treated it with the same care we'd give a luxury villa. That philosophy hasn't changed.

Today, we work across residential construction, commercial spaces, and interior design — always bringing the same energy, attention to detail, and client-first mindset that defined our very first project.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

From our first project to where we stand today.

2022
Vihaan is Founded
Three construction professionals come together in Chennai with a shared vision — to build better, more honestly. Our first office: a rented desk and a lot of ambition.
2022
First Project: Boutique Retail Store
Completed a 1,800 sq.ft. premium retail fit-out in T. Nagar — ahead of schedule and to glowing client feedback. The benchmark was set.
2023
Expanding Into Residential
Took on our first residential projects — the Garden View Residence and the Luxury Villa Interiors — marking our entry into full home construction and design.
2024
Largest Project to Date
Delivered the Sunrise Township Complex — a mixed-use residential and commercial development, our most ambitious project yet. All 15 units handed over on time.
2025+
Growing, Not Just Scaling
Focused on thoughtful growth — more projects, a deeper team, and an unwavering commitment to the quality and care that defines Vihaan.
